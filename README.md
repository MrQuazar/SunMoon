# 🌱 Bloom or Doom

### A Cooperative Multiplayer Game About Balance

**Bloom or Doom** is a **2-player cooperative multiplayer game** created
during a **3‑day game jam at Kingston University hosted by Tencent**.\
The theme of the jam was **Equilibrium**, and our game explores balance
through cooperative gameplay mechanics where players must manage
opposing forces to restore life to a dying planet.

Players take control of two satellites representing the **Sun** and the
**Moon**, working together to transform a barren planet into a
flourishing ecosystem before time runs out.

# 🎮 Game Overview

The Blorbians of **Bleblornia** lost their home planet to a catastrophic
calamity.\
Now they have discovered a new barren world with the potential for life.

Two satellites orbit this planet:

☀️ **Sun Satellite** - Encourages plant growth - Turns barren land
fertile - Too much sunlight causes dangerous carnivorous overgrowth

🌙 **Moon Satellite** - Reverses dangerous plant overgrowth - Converts
carnivorous plants into peaceful flowers - Too much moonlight drains the
land, leaving it barren again

Players must maintain **perfect equilibrium** to restore the planet.

---

# 🛰️ Core Gameplay Mechanics

## Sun & Moon Balance

Each player controls a satellite with **opposite environmental
effects**.

The challenge lies in maintaining balance: - Too much Sun → Overgrowth -
Too much Moon → Barren land

Players must coordinate their movements to create the ideal environment
for flowers to bloom.

---

## Satellite Malfunctions

Satellites occasionally malfunction, creating chaotic events such as:

- ☄️ Asteroids
- 🔥 Solar flares
- ⚡ Overcharged beams

These disrupt the environment and require players to quickly adapt.

---

## Water Stabilization

Players can move their satellites toward **water regions** on the planet
to stabilize their systems and protect progress.

This mechanic adds strategic positioning to gameplay.

---

# 🌘 NFC Eclipse Mechanic (Star Feature)

One of the most unique features of the game is **NFC interaction**.

Players can **tap their phones together** during gameplay to merge the
Sun and Moon satellites into a powerful **Eclipse Satellite**.

The Eclipse fires a **perfect beam** that instantly converts land into
blooming flowers.

This mechanic encourages **physical player interaction**, bringing
people together in the real world while playing together in the game.

---

# 📱 Accessibility Features

Accessibility was an important design goal for the project.

The game includes:

- 📲 **Gyroscope controls** as an alternative to joystick input
- 🎮 **On-screen joystick controls**
- 🗣 **Dialogue narration with text on screen**

These features allow players to interact with the game in ways that best
suit their preferences.

---

# 🌐 Multiplayer System

The game uses a **Node.js server** for online multiplayer.

### Multiplayer Flow

1.  A player hosts a room
2.  A **unique room code** is generated
3.  A second player joins using the code
4.  Host controls **Sun Satellite**
5.  Joiner controls **Moon Satellite**

This allows quick matchmaking during live play sessions.
